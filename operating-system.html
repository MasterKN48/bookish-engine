Questions

Good to know.

Who is this course for?
Developers and CS students who can already write and run programs, but want to understand what happens underneath them: how a process is born, how the CPU is shared, why concurrency is hard, and how memory is stretched across many programs at once.
How is a chapter laid out?
Each chapter opens with a short introduction, then works through its topic one section at a time. Every section pairs a drawn page with a written explanation, so you can take in the picture first and then read the detail.
How long does it take?
About 11 hours of reading in all, across 296 sections. A chapter takes between 13 and 19 minutes, so one fits comfortably into a study session.
