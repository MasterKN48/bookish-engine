Questions

Good to know.

Who is this course for?
Students working through a university databases syllabus, and engineers who want to know why a query is slow, why an index was ignored, or what really happens between BEGIN and COMMIT. Everywhere the theory has a practical side, the course shows both.
How is a chapter laid out?
Each chapter opens with a short introduction, then works through its topic one section at a time. Every section pairs a drawn page with a written explanation, so you can take in the picture first and then read the detail. Some chapters (4 in all) also include an interactive figure you can step through.
How long does it take?
About 12 hours of reading in all, across 239 sections. A chapter takes between 18 and 24 minutes, so one fits comfortably into a study session.
