Under the hood

What the AI is actually doing

Four jobs, in order. Get the first one wrong and the rest do not matter.

  1. 01

    Drafting a contents page, not a task list

    It settles the chapters first and orders them by dependency, the shell before containers, containers before orchestration. A plan in the wrong order is useless however good the individual days are.

  2. 02

    Sizing the subject honestly

    Ask for one data structure and you get a tight week; ask for a whole role and it uses every day you offered.

  3. 03

    Reaching into our own problem library

    Anything algorithmic is built from problems that already live here, so a task opens on the page where you watch the algorithm move, not a link to somewhere else.

  4. 04

    Writing tasks you can start

    “Learn Docker” is a heading. “Write a Dockerfile for a Node app and cut its image size with a multi-stage build” is something you can begin now.

Life happens

Miss a day and nothing breaks.

Unfinished work becomes backlog and waits, grouped by the day it was meant to happen. Nothing is rescheduled, nothing expires, and the plan keeps the shape you agreed to.

Yes. You read the whole plan before it starts. Delete what you already know and add what you want.
