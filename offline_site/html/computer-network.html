Questions

Good to know.

Who is this course for?
Developers who already know what a server, a frontend and an HTTP request are, but want to see what is really happening underneath. The notes are student-friendly, so it also suits anyone meeting networking for the first time.
How is a chapter laid out?
Each chapter opens with a short introduction, then works through its topic one section at a time. Every section pairs a drawn page with a written explanation, so you can take in the picture first and then read the detail. Some chapters (4 in all) also include an interactive figure you can step through.
How long does it take?
About 5 hours of reading in all, across 231 sections. A chapter takes between 5 and 20 minutes, so one fits comfortably into a study session.
