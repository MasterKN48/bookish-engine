Question 7 of 30 · Time and work

One pipe fills a tank in 6 hours. Another empties it in 9 hours. With both open, how long does the tank take to fill?

A12 hoursB15 hoursC18 hoursD24 hours

Correct · worked answer

Each hour the tank gains 1/6 and loses 1/9, a net 1/18. So it fills in 18 hours.

Questions

Good to know.

Is BookishEngine free?
You can start for free. The first DSA track, the video courses and the practice sheets are open to everyone. One plan, from ₹499 a month, unlocks every course, aptitude mock and roadmap. AI interviews are bought separately, from ₹99 each.
What happened to Chai Visual?
It grew. Chai Visual started as animated DSA lessons, and those are all still here as the visual courses. BookishEngine is the name for everything around them now: notes, videos, practice, mocks and interviews.
Who is it for?
Students preparing for placements, engineers getting ready to switch jobs, and colleges or companies preparing a whole batch together.
How are the AI interviews marked?
Every round is marked against a written rubric for that problem. Your debrief explains each score and quotes the parts of your work it is based on, so the feedback is specific and easy to act on.
Do I need to install anything?
No. Everything runs in your browser, including the codebase and editor in the coding interview.
