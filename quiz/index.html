Caching

Scenario

A link shortener serves 50,000 redirects a second. Most of them hit the same few thousand links, and the database is at 90% CPU.

What is the cheapest change that takes most of the load off the database?

  • 1Add read replicas
  • 2Cache the hot links in memory
  • 3Shard the database by user
  • 4Move to a bigger server

Correct

A few thousand hot links fit easily in memory, so a cache answers most redirects without touching the database. Replicas and sharding help too, at far more cost.

How it works

Built to teach, not just to score.

    Commit to an answer and see right away why it is right, and why the tempting ones are not.

Questions

Good to know.

Is the quiz free?
One quiz in every subject is free for anyone signed in. The rest, and Ask a follow-up, come with the plan, from ₹499 a month. During a learning month everything is open.
How is a quiz marked?
Each question is all or nothing: on a choose-two, both picks must be right; on a match or an order, every item must be in place. Your score is how many of the fifteen you got right.
Can I stop halfway?
Yes. Close the tab and the quiz waits for you with your answers kept. Or finish early and get a score on what you answered.
Where do the questions come from?
From our own chapters and lessons in each subject, checked twice before they go live. If one looks wrong, use the flag on the question and we will look at it.
